# Device and Equipment Policy

**Owner:** IT Operations · **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-01 · **Applies to:** all staff and contractors

## Standard issue

Every full-time employee receives one primary device on their start date. Contractors
receive a device only when the engagement exceeds 90 days; shorter engagements use
the contractor's own hardware under the BYOD rules in `04-remote-access-policy`.

| Role family | Standard device | Refresh cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | MacBook Pro 16" or Dell Precision 5690 | 36 months |
| Design | MacBook Pro 16" | 36 months |
| Sales, Success, Marketing | MacBook Air 15" or Dell Latitude 7450 | 48 months |
| Finance, People, Legal | Dell Latitude 7450 | 48 months |
| Warehouse and field | Getac F110 rugged tablet | 60 months |

Refresh is automatic. IT opens a replacement ticket 30 days before the cycle ends;
staff do not need to request it.

## Accessories

Each employee may claim, once per refresh cycle, without approval:

- One external monitor up to 27 inches
- One keyboard and one pointing device
- One headset up to $180
- One laptop stand or docking station

Anything above these limits, or a second monitor, needs a manager's written approval
in the ticket. Requests above $500 additionally need Finance approval — see
`06-procurement-and-approvals`.

## Damage, loss, and theft

Report a lost or stolen device **within 4 hours of noticing**, at any hour. This is
the one IT matter with no business-hours restriction, because remote wipe is only
reliable before the device is powered off and taken off the network.

- Accidental damage: repaired or replaced at company cost, no questions, first two
  incidents in any 24-month window.
- Third incident in 24 months: manager review before replacement.
- Loss or theft: replaced at company cost. A police report is required only when
  the device held customer data, which applies to Engineering, Success, and Finance.
- Deliberate damage or sale of company equipment: recovered through payroll and
  handled under the disciplinary process owned by People Ops.

## Personal use

Company devices may be used for personal browsing, personal email, and personal
media. They may not be used to store personal financial records, to run a
second business, or by anyone other than the assigned employee — including family.

## Return

Devices are returned on the last working day. The offboarding steps and the
deadline for returning a device after remote termination are in
`07-onboarding-and-offboarding`. Devices not returned within 21 days of the last
working day are invoiced to the employee at replacement cost.
