# Remote Work Policy

**Owner:** People Operations · **Last reviewed:** 2026-01-20

This policy covers *where and when* people work. The technical rules for connecting
to company systems from outside the office are separate and live in
`04-remote-access-policy`.

## Baseline

The company is hybrid by default. Office-based roles attend the office **two days
per week**, chosen by the team, not by the individual. Teams publish their anchor
days in the team handbook and change them no more than once per quarter.

Fully remote contracts exist but are approved case by case by the Head of People,
and are noted in the employment contract. There is no path to convert a hybrid
contract to fully remote by request alone; it requires a role change.

## Equipment stipend

Remote and hybrid staff receive a home-office stipend:

| | Amount | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Initial setup | $750 | Once, on joining |
| Ongoing | $45 | Monthly, with payroll |
| Internet contribution | $30 | Monthly, with payroll |

The ongoing and internet amounts are paid automatically. They are taxable in most
jurisdictions; Finance handles the treatment and staff do not need to file anything.

The initial $750 is claimed through an expense report within the first 60 days.
Unclaimed after 60 days, it lapses — this is the most common stipend question IT
receives and the answer is that Finance cannot reinstate it.

## Working hours and overlap

Core overlap is **10:00–15:00 in the employee's registered time zone**, with the
requirement that every employee overlaps at least 3 hours with their direct
manager. Requests to move a registered time zone by more than 3 hours need
manager and People Ops approval, because they affect the overlap rule.

## Working from another country

Working from a country other than the registered one is allowed for up to
**30 calendar days per rolling 12 months**, with manager notification only. Beyond
30 days it becomes a tax and employment-law matter and requires People Ops and
Legal approval, which takes 4 to 6 weeks. Do not book travel first.

Some countries are excluded regardless of duration due to sanctions or data
residency rules. The current exclusion list is held by Legal and is not published
in this handbook — ask People Ops.

## Office access

Badge access covers 06:00–21:00 on weekdays. Weekend or overnight access is granted
per request, expires after 14 days, and requires a manager's approval in the
ticket.
