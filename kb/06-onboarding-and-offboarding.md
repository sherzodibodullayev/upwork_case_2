# Onboarding and Offboarding

**Owner:** IT Operations with People Operations · **Last reviewed:** 2026-02-05

## Onboarding

People Ops files the new-starter request. IT needs **5 business days' notice** to
have a device imaged and accounts ready for day one. Less than 5 days and the
starter begins on a loaner machine, which is workable but means a second setup
later.

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| −5 | Request received, device imaged, accounts created but disabled |
| −1 | Accounts enabled, welcome email to personal address |
| 1 | 45-minute IT setup session, 10:00 local — MFA enrolment, VPN, tools |
| 3 | Security awareness module due |
| 14 | Access review with manager: is anything missing or excessive |

The day-one setup session is booked automatically. Starters who miss it can book a
replacement slot; IT holds four slots each afternoon for this.

Access is granted by role template, not by copying a colleague's access. Copying
another person's permissions is how excess access spreads, and IT will decline the
request even when a manager asks for it directly.

## Offboarding

People Ops files the leaver request. IT needs **2 business days' notice** for a
planned departure.

| Departure type | Access revoked | Device returned |
|---|---|---|
| Planned resignation | End of last working day, 18:00 | Last working day |
| End of contract | End of last working day, 18:00 | Last working day |
| Immediate termination | Within 1 hour of the People Ops request | Courier within 5 business days |
| Death in service | Handled individually by People Ops and Legal | Handled individually |

For remote leavers, IT sends a prepaid courier box. A device not returned within 21
days is invoiced at replacement cost, as set out in
`01-device-and-equipment-policy`.

## Mailbox and files after departure

- Mailbox: converted to a shared mailbox, delegated to the manager for **90 days**,
  then deleted
- Personal drive: transferred to the manager for **90 days**, then deleted
- Chat history: retained 12 months under the company retention schedule, not
  accessible to the manager
- Automatic forwarding to a manager's mailbox is **not** set up, in any
  circumstance. Delegated access is the supported route, and the difference matters
  legally.

## Contractors

Contractor access is granted with a hard expiry matching the contract end date, set
at creation. Extending a contract requires extending the expiry explicitly — access
does not roll over, and a contractor whose expiry passes on a Friday will find
themselves locked out on Monday with no warning email.

A former contractor or employee who still has working access is a security
incident, not a ticket. Report it as described in `05-security-incident-response`.
